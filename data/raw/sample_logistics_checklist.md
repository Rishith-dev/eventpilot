# Event Logistics Checklist

(Synthetic sample for testing. Replace with your own sanitised documents.)

## 4 weeks before

- Confirm the venue booking in writing and collect the permission letter from the administration.
- Fix the final date, time slots and room allocation for each activity.
- Shortlist two caterers and ask for quotes per plate.
- Form the volunteer teams: registration, hospitality, stage, technical, discipline.

## 2 weeks before

- Finalise the caterer, menu and headcount estimate. Confirm whether the menu must be vegetarian.
- Book the sound system, projector, microphones and extension boards.
- Order banners, standees, certificates and participant ID cards.
- Share the volunteer schedule and shift timings with everyone.

## 1 week before

- Do a full walkthrough of the venue with the stage and technical teams.
- Print the registration lists and prepare the help desk with pens, tape and extra printouts.
- Confirm the first-aid kit, drinking water points and a safety contact person.
- Brief volunteers on crowd management and emergency exits.

## Event day

- Volunteers arrive 90 minutes early. Set up the registration desk and test all audio and video.
- Check food delivery 45 minutes before lunch and keep separate counters to avoid queues.
- Keep a small cash float for last-minute purchases and record every expense with a bill.

## After the event

- Collect feedback forms and thank sponsors within 48 hours.
- Return rented equipment, settle all vendor payments and write the final expense report.
