## Overview

TechNova 2025 was a two-day technology festival held in March with 520 registered participants. It included a 12-hour hackathon, three workshops, a quiz and a closing ceremony. The total budget was Rs 2,10,000.

## Income

- Title sponsor: Rs 75,000
- Two Gold sponsors: Rs 40,000 each
- Registration fees: Rs 55,000

## Expenses

- Food: Rs 78,000
- Venue and equipment: Rs 40,000
- Prizes: Rs 35,000
- Printing and publicity: Rs 30,000
- Miscellaneous: Rs 15,000

## Food and catering

We hired a local caterer and served lunch to about 450 people on each day at Rs 180 per plate. The menu was vegetarian only, which avoided complaints. We opened two separate food counters so the queue never crossed ten minutes. Tea, coffee and snacks were served during the evening hackathon. On day two the caterer ran short of rice for 40 people, so next time we should order 10 percent extra and confirm the headcount 48 hours before.

## Marketing

Most registrations came from Instagram reels and posters in class groups. A countdown series in the last ten days gave the best response. Email outreach to other colleges brought very few participants.

## What worked

- The sponsor stall in the main foyer got strong footfall.
- Volunteers wearing the same colour t-shirt were easy to find.

## What to improve

- Start sponsor outreach 8 weeks ahead instead of 5.
- Appoint one person to track every expense bill from day one.
